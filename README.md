# Clothes-FIT-APP
A personal clothing fit planning web app for checking clothing measurements, so you know your size and fit without a tailor, and every time you go shopping you get the exact fit that shows off the style and aesthetic you want, down to the detail.
# Fit Roadmap

**Your fit. Your rules.**

Fit Roadmap is a personal clothing-fit planning web app. Enter your body and the fit you like, and it gives you the exact garment measurements to look for in any store, or to hand to a tailor, so nothing ends up too tight or too loose.

It is built for real shopping, including mitumba, thrift, and boutique finds where size tags are unreliable.

---

## Features

### 1. Personal Fit Dashboard
- Personalized welcome with an editable nickname
- Quick access to every section of the app
- A 3-step fit journey tracker: set your fit, save a favorite, build your diary

### 2. Fit Profile
- Get estimated measurements from your height, weight, and build
- Add your real measurements for better accuracy
- Treat estimates as a starting range (about ±2 cm)

### 3. Camera-First Garment Checker
- Photograph a garment with your phone camera, or choose an existing image
- Place a bank card or sheet of paper flat on the item as a size reference
- Tap the ends of the reference to calibrate, then measure the garment from the photo
- Or skip the photo and enter garment measurements manually

### 4. Fit Verdict
- Compare an item's measurements against your target fit
- Get a clear verdict on whether the piece will work for you

### 5. Size Tag Conversion
- See the tags to pick off the rack across regional sizing systems
- Ignore unreliable labels and shop by measurements instead

### 6. Saved Favorites
- Save measurements from clothes you already love
- Keep them as your personal library for future shopping

### 7. Fit Diary
- Log how purchased items actually fit
- Learn from real purchases and refine your choices over time

## Getting Started

### Run Locally

1. Clone this repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/fit-roadmap.git
   ```

2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

No build process is required.

### Mobile Usage

Open the app in a mobile browser. On compatible devices, the garment checker can open your camera directly. Otherwise, choose a photo from your gallery.

## Privacy

Your information stays on your device. Fit Roadmap saves your profile, favorites, and diary in your browser's local storage and does not upload them to a server. Because of this, data does not sync between devices, and clearing your browser data will erase it.

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which is the only external request it makes.
