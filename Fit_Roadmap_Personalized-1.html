Start with your camera

Photograph the garment and estimate measurements directly on the image — no tape measure needed.

Lay the item flat and put a bank card or a sheet of paper on it, lying flat. Take the photo from above (a slight tilt is fine, the app corrects for it). Tap the four corners of the reference: start at one end of a long edge, go along it, then around. Then pick a measurement and tap its two ends. Drag any point to fine-tune it. Expect about 1 cm of error.

Or enter measurements manually

How to measure an item

Lay it flat and smooth it out. Chest: straight across from armpit seam to armpit seam. Length: from the top of the shoulder beside the collar down to the hem. Shoulders: seam to seam across the back. Sleeve: shoulder seam to cuff. Waist: across the top of the waistband, fastened. Seat: across the widest point below the fly. Inseam: crotch seam to hem along the inside leg. Stretchy fabric measures smaller flat than it wears.
